Meet your private desktop companion

MyPerro can react to your activity without reading what you type.

  • Counts, never content.
    Only activity totals and pointer movement are processed in memory.
  • Free and local.
    No account, sign-up, subscription or cloud service is required.
  • Only your pet stays on the desktop.
    Click the pet, then use F, W, P, R or S. There are no overlay buttons.
  • You stay in control.
    Input reactions, notifications and startup can be disabled at any time.

Your operating system may show an accessibility permission prompt.
