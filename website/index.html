II

Behaviour, not decoration

Small actions with a clear reason.

A single behaviour director decides what the pet is doing. Care, play, movement, and reactions no longer compete for the same moment.

  1. 01

    Moves with intent

    The pet looks, sets off, walks or runs in the correct direction, and settles at the destination. Play can become a genuine tumble instead of a sliding pose.

  2. 02

    Notices the rhythm of work

    Typing, pointer movement, scrolling, breaks, and quiet periods produce restrained reactions. Reduced motion keeps the same meaning with calmer poses.

  3. 03

    Needs ordinary care

    Food, water, happiness, and energy change over time. Feed with F, give water with W, play with P, or begin a one-minute rest with R.

  4. 04

    Speaks like a companion

    Short barks, purrs, sips, eating sounds, and sleepy murmurs answer visible actions. Sound level and quiet mode remain under your control.

  5. 05

    Fits the desk

    Resize from 65 to 200 percent, adjust opacity and markings, use edge peek, or turn off always-on-top when the desktop needs more room.

  6. 06

    Helps without becoming a dashboard

    Optional water and stretch reminders, a focus timer, personal notes, and local update checks live behind the companion rather than around it.

A plain privacy promise

Activity can shape behaviour. Content never leaves your hands.

When input reactions are enabled, MyPerro reduces movement, clicks, scroll distance, and typing to anonymous counts at capture time. It does not receive typed text, key names, clipboard contents, screenshots, browsing history, or document contents.

III

Before installation

Useful details.

Is MyPerro free?

Yes. MyPerro is open-source software with no subscription, purchase, advertising, account, or locked premium tier.

Does it read what I type?

No. Native input code discards key identity and emits only a count. Typed text cannot be reconstructed by the app.

How are settings opened?

Right-click the companion or press S while it is focused. The system tray menu provides the same route.

Will the pet block other windows?

Transparent pixels pass clicks through to the desktop. Size, opacity, edge peek, reduced motion, and always-on-top can all be adjusted.
