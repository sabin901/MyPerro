Good to know

Questions before your new roommate moves in?

Is MyPerro really free?

Yes. MyPerro is open-source software with no subscription, license purchase, account, advertising, or locked premium tier.

Does it read what I type?

No. When input reactions are enabled, native code discards key identity immediately and emits only a count. Typed text can never be reconstructed.

How do I open Settings if only the pet is visible?

Right-click the companion or press S while it is focused. F feeds, W gives water, P plays, and R starts a one-minute rest.

Will it get in the way?

Transparent pixels pass clicks through to your desktop. You can resize the pet, lower opacity, use peek mode, disable always-on-top, or pause reminders with quiet mode.
